Otto's Daily Byte

A daily AI & tech news show, anchored by Otto – an animated otter.

Every day Otto covers the biggest AI and technology stories in about five minutes, with every story sourced and linked. Each story is also published as its own YouTube Short.

Otto News is the small private tool the channel owner uses to upload those videos to the channel's own YouTube account. It is not offered to other users.
