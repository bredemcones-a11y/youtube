Privacy policy – Otto News

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Otto News is a private tool that uploads videos for the YouTube channel "Otto's Daily Byte". It is used only by the channel owner, on the owner's own computer.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's permission (Google sign-in), the app uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload videos and set their title, description, tags and publish time (scope youtube.upload), and
  • read the name of the connected channel to confirm the right account is used (scope youtube.readonly).

The app does not read, collect or store any other Google account data, and it never accesses data of viewers or other users.

How the data is stored

The only thing kept is the sign-in token, stored in a local file on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs. No analytics, no tracking, no advertising.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file removes all stored data.
